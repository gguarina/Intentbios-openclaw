# Cold Read: *The Ash Cycle, Book N: Title* (branch @ sha)

## 1. Verdict
3–5 sentences: what kind of book it is, what works best, what fails, would you read on and why.

## 2. Scores (1–10, one line of evidence each)
- Voice
- Character differentiation
- Narrative drive (name the chapters that move and the ones that drift)
- Clarity
- Emotional payoff
- Prose (quote one excellent and one weak line)
- Originality

## 3. Voice-alone test
Three pairs of unattributed dialogue lines from different speakers. For each: who speaks, how you could tell, **Pass/Fail**.

## 4. Remaining tics (exact quotes)
Recurring moves shared across characters or narration, each with 2–4 quoted instances and chapter numbers.

## 5. Confusions
Where a first reader gets lost (names, timelines, mechanics), with chapter.

## 6. Top five fixes
Ordered by impact; each one sentence, concrete.
